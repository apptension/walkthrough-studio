                        How a video gets made
                        
          
                          
            
                        1. BriefWho it’s for and what they should learn.
                          2. 
            
                        2. ScriptApprove what’s said and shown. Cheapest stage to change.
                          3. 
            
                        3. DirectionColors, logo, feature focus, auto zoom, frame, cursor and transitions.
                          4. 
            
                        4. RecordThe app is captured live, following the script.
                          5. 
            
                        5. CutWatch the render, give feedback, approve.
                          6. 
            
                        6. DeliverExport MP4, captions, chapters, poster, plus square, vertical and GIF cuts.
                          7. 
          
                        
          
                        The stage bar under the header shows where this video is. The highlighted stage is the next one to act on; click any stage to open it.
                        

          
                        Where things are
                        
          
                        
            
                        Left · Render
                        What needs doing, and the button that does it. Live progress and the step queue.
                        
            
                        Left · Versions
                        Saved and approved cuts. Play, compare or restore any of them.
                        
            
                        Centre · Program
                        The rendered video. Editor plays the whole cut with one timeline: clips, transition markers, a speed row and a zoom row. Click anything to edit it in the Inspector; drag across the speed or zoom row to pick a stretch.
                        
            
                        Right · Feedback
                        Tell the agent what to change; review its preview, then approve or discard.
                        
            
                        Right · Motion, Review, Deliver
                        Plate motion and transitions, quality checks and chapters, the handoff pack.
                        
          
                        

          
                        Words you’ll see
                        
          
                        
            
                        Plate
                        A designed brand scene: the opener, title cards, the close.
                        
            
                        Chapter
                        A section of the live tour. Becomes a YouTube chapter.
                        
            
                        Out of date
                        Sources changed since the last render; Render to see them.
                        
            
                        Checkpoint
                        An automatic save from each render, hidden in Versions by default.
                        
            
                        Handoff pack
                        Everything the client receives, as one zip.
